Who We Are

Lakyaa Creation & Academy

Welcome to Lakyaa Creation & Academy — a place where tradition meets artistry. We are a passionate team of skilled artisans and designers based in Valvettiththurai, Sri Lanka, dedicated to bringing beauty and elegance to every creation.

From intricate aari embroidery to stunning bridal mahendi, silk thread jewellery to tailored saree blouses — we pour our heart into every piece we craft. Our work is inspired by the rich heritage of South Indian textile arts, reimagined with a modern sensibility.

Beyond creation, we are also an academy — nurturing the next generation of artisans through skill development programs in aari work, embroidery, henna, and more.
